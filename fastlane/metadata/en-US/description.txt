Odometer is the maintenance log that lives in the glovebox of your phone. Log a fill-up at the pump or a service in the waiting room, and it tells you what is due next, when, and what the car really costs.

Type the reading off the dash and the digits roll over like a real odometer. Every reading sharpens your pace, and the pace turns "due at 58,390 miles" into "about February 3rd".

WHAT IS DUE
• Oil, tires, filters, brake fluid, coolant, transmission, plugs, battery, timing belt, wipers, plus your own jobs
• Counted from the last time each one was done, in miles and months
• Intervals you can change per car, and jobs you can switch off
• Overdue, due soon, and a gauge showing how much of the interval is used up

LOG IT IN SECONDS
• Services with shop or DIY, parts, labor and a note
• Fill-ups with mpg or L/100km from one full tank to the next
• Insurance, registration, parking, tolls and washes

THE REAL COST
• Cost per month and per mile since you got the car
• The last twelve months by month and by category
• Fuel economy trend and cost per mile at the pump

THE GLOVEBOX
• Insurance renewal, registration, inspection and warranty dates, with a countdown
• Optional reminder two weeks before, as a local notification

THE HISTORY A BUYER WANTS
• A clean service history PDF with every job, mileage, shop and cost, ready to share when you sell

Miles or kilometres, gallons or litres, your currency. As many cars as you own. Export everything as CSV.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

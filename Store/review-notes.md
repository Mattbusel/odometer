# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

HOW TO USE: Tap "Add a vehicle", type the year, make and model and the current mileage, save. The Car tab shows the odometer (tap it to type a new reading), what is due, and the glovebox dates. The Due tab lists every maintenance job with its interval; tap one to log it as done. Fuel logs fill-ups and shows economy. Log holds services and expenses. Costs shows the money by month and category. "Service history" on the Car tab renders a PDF you can share. The optional "Remind me 14 days before" switch in the vehicle editor asks for notification permission only when turned on.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. One non-consumable, "Odometer Pro" (com.mattbusel.odometer.pro), unlocks more than one vehicle, the Costs tab, the service history PDF and the CSV export. One vehicle with its schedule, reminders, fuel, log and glovebox dates is free forever. To see the paywall: add a vehicle, then tap the Costs tab and tap "See Odometer Pro"; or tap "Service history" on the Car tab; or tap the lock button at the end of the vehicle strip at the top of the Car tab. Restore purchase is on the paywall and under the SEE button on the Odometer Pro card in Settings (gear icon, top right of the Car tab). No subscription, no ads.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored in a JSON file in the app's Documents folder on the device. Local notifications only, and only if the user turns a reminder on. The share sheet is used for the PDF and CSV exports the user requests.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Odometer is a personal car maintenance log: mileage readings, a service schedule with intervals, fuel economy, expenses, cost of ownership and a shareable service history. General audience; rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials. A new install starts with an empty garage; add one vehicle and its mileage.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None, apart from Apple's StoreKit for the one in-app purchase. No other network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. Built with SwiftUI, Swift Charts, Foundation and UserNotifications (local reminders only).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Miles or kilometres, gallons or litres and the currency symbol are user settings.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. All art, text and code are my own work.
